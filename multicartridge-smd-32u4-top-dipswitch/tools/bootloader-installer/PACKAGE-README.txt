P2000T BOOTLOADER INSTALLER
===========================

Run p2000t-bootloader-installer.exe and follow the menu. Keep all files in this
folder together. No separate AVRDUDE installation is required.

Keep the cartridge out of the P2000T. Do not connect the cartridge USB port
while the USBasp powers the ISP contacts. Hold the ISP contacts steady until
the program says they may be released.

The included avrdude.exe and avrdude.conf are unmodified files from AVRDUDE 8.2:
https://github.com/avrdudes/avrdude/releases/tag/v8.2

AVRDUDE is a separate program distributed under GNU GPL version 2. Its licence
